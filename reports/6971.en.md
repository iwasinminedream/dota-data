- Evidence that this is noise:
  - `dump-noise.md` is a control dump of the same build 6971. In its `cl_script_reload` section it has the same +17/−17 lines.   - If the constant had really been removed, the values of the following `ACT_DOTA_*` would have shifted by −1. There are no such shifts in `api-changes.txt`.
- Confidence in the conclusion is high: it rests on a direct comparison with the control dump and the dump history.
