### For custom game developers

- Nothing new, removed, or changed in the Lua API, Panorama, events, modifiers, and console variables.
- Existing addons should not be broken by this update.
- Do not hardcode the numeric values of `ACT_DOTA_*` and `muerta_debut_satyr_*` / `radiant_melee_mars_shieldbash`. These numbers are assigned dynamically and may change even between runs of the same build. Use the constant names, for example `ACT_DOTA_CAST_ABILITY_1`.
- If you compare dumps automatically, add a filter for these constants so they do not produce false positives.
